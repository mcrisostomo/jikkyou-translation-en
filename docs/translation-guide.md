# Translation Guide

## Status

Initial documentation placeholder. English terminology and editorial conventions will be established as the game's text is researched.

## Initial principles

- Translate faithfully while respecting the game's context and space constraints.
- Keep names, team terminology, menus, and gameplay messages consistent.
- Record uncertain readings and unresolved context instead of silently guessing.
- Preserve control codes and formatting markers exactly until their behavior is validated.
- Write project documentation and research notes in English.
