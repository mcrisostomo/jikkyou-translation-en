# AI-Assisted Development

This project is developed with extensive assistance from artificial intelligence. ChatGPT and Codex are used as development assistants throughout the project, including for:

- Research and technical investigation.
- Reverse engineering of game data, code, formats, and behavior.
- Development and maintenance of scripts and other tooling.
- Technical and project documentation.
- Troubleshooting, debugging, and analysis of unexpected results.
- Assistance with translation work, terminology, and language review.

The human maintainer remains responsible for directing the project, defining its goals and scope, making technical and editorial decisions, and reviewing and validating the results. AI assistance does not replace human judgment or project ownership.

Results produced with AI assistance are not automatically considered correct. They must be independently reviewed, tested where applicable, and validated against the project's evidence and requirements before being accepted or relied upon. This includes technical findings, scripts, documentation, and translation suggestions.

This document describes the project's use of AI assistance. It does not assert that any specific test, reverse-engineering result, script, documentation item, or translation has been completed merely because AI assistance is available for that activity.
